Bufan Jiang

I am a Project Specialist (Research Assistant) at Dong Fureng Institute of Economic and Social Development, Wuhan University, China. My work involves both the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS) and the China Health and Economy Longitudinal Study (CHELS).

My research interests lie in health economics, labour economics, and the economics of education. More specifically, my research focuses on how individual and household behaviour is shaped by social development and public policy. I am particularly interested in families’ fertility and education decisions, the behaviour of patients and physicians in medical markets (including physician-induced demand), and demographic change in China.
